## The product

SYBG, short for "Since You've Been Gone", is a SaaS product from 10X Ventures. It lets website owners publish personalized product announcements on their site, inside their product, or by email, without writing code. Setup takes two steps: add a website, then create a widget and style its colors, appearance, and notification style to match the site. A help center covers accounts, plans, team members, and analytics.

## My part

I worked remotely with a US-based team of full-stack engineers on Blitz.js, a React and Node.js framework built on Next.js, with PostgreSQL, Prisma, Tailwind CSS and SCSS.

This was the second SaaS product I built on React and Node.js. My biggest piece of work was billing: I integrated Stripe for payment processing and user subscription management, working with guidance from the team on the parts that were new to me.

Across 10X Ventures, the Stripe subscription work I was part of grew recurring revenue by 20%.

## Where it led

Payment integrations run through the rest of my work too: Paystack billing at AluSoft Technologies, which cut manual billing by 50%, and payment API integrations in my freelance projects. [FirstX](/work/firstx) was built by the same team on the same stack.

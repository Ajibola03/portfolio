## The product

ChurchPorch is a SaaS product from AluSoft Technologies for running a church of any size. Its main features:

- A customizable member database, with fields each church defines for itself
- Program automation, with contributions from members
- Financial reports on funds received and spent over a date range
- Media for events, devotions, and announcements
- A tree of a church and its branches

A companion mobile app gives members and administrators the same tools on their phones.

## My part

I worked with a small team of full-stack developers in Nigeria. The web platform runs on Laravel with MySQL, styled with Bootstrap and plain CSS.

The project was large for the team's size, so I played a central role at every stage of development, from planning through launch. I also contributed a large share of the companion mobile app, so members and administrators got a consistent experience on the web and on their phones.
